About the project

Radar V2 is an independent project focused on analyzing publicly available digital advertising data, identifying patterns in ad activity, campaign duration, creative variation, and market trends.

The project aims to develop automated methods for collecting, organizing, and comparing advertising indicators across European markets, with a focus on digital products and online education.

The resulting insights support market research, competitive analysis, and commercial product development.

Research focus

Advertising activity

Comparing publicly observable ad activity and campaign timing.

Creative patterns

Examining variations in messaging and creative approaches across advertisers.

Market trends

Exploring public signals relevant to digital products and online education.

Responsible analysis

The project focuses on legitimately accessible public information. Ad duration, creative volume, and reach estimates are indirect indicators, not proof of revenue or profitability. Third-party API access is subject to provider terms and eligibility criteria.
